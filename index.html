// about me

Wearing Many Hats

I'm a multi-role professional who thrives at the intersection of public service, technology, and exploration. By day I keep the Kittitas County Prosecutor's Office running smoothly. When the call comes in, I investigate as a certified Deputy Coroner, a role I've held for over 16 years, bringing answers to families during the hardest moments of their lives. On the side, I keep the taps flowing as IT Admin at Iron Horse Brewery.

When I'm not doing any of that, I'm volunteering as a firefighter with Kittitas Valley Fire & Rescue, flying drones with my FAA Part 107 certificate, building custom PCs, self-hosting media servers, tinkering with 3D printers, or planning the next international adventure with my wife Jeanne and our four cats.

I believe in continuous learning, serving the community, and that the best craft beer is the one you're sharing with friends.
