Fitness Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

This policy covers Mission Control Fitness, a private, personal-use fitness integration operated by Jacob Schroeder. It is not a public fitness service and is not operated by WHOOP, Peloton, or Withings. Device connections collect data only after the account owner authorizes access.

Information collected

Depending on the connected service and permissions granted, the integration reads workout dates, types, duration, activity and effort metrics (including Peloton Strive Score and WHOOP Strain when available), sleep duration and related sleep metrics, recovery data, and weight measurements. It also stores manually entered fitness records, goals, commitments, check-ins, rescheduling history, and accountability or emergency-override events. Provider identifiers and authorization tokens are used to maintain connections.

How information is used

Information is used to display personal fitness trends, track progress, avoid duplicate records, support planning and accountability, and evaluate user-configured activity and weight-recording requirements for access to the owner's assistant. This integration is not intended to diagnose or treat medical conditions.

Storage and security

Fitness records are stored in a private database on the operator's computer. Connection credentials are stored separately in permission-restricted local files. This credential storage is not an encrypted credential vault. Access is restricted to the operator and authorized services. Local backups may contain retained records. No security measure guarantees absolute protection.

Sharing and service providers

Fitness information is not sold, used for advertising, or published on this website. Connected fitness providers process authorization and data requests under their own policies. Enabled reminders send limited fitness and accountability information to the owner's private Telegram conversation, where Telegram processes and retains messages under its policies. The scheduled sync and reminder scripts do not use AI. When the owner asks the connected assistant to analyze fitness information, relevant information may be processed by the assistant's configured AI service providers under their policies. Hosting providers may process ordinary request metadata when this public policy page is visited; health records are not part of this page.

Retention and deletion

Fitness records are retained until the operator deletes them; there is no automatic retention expiry. Archiving a record is not permanent deletion. Disconnecting or revoking provider access stops future authorized retrieval but does not by itself erase previously imported data. The operator can remove local records and connection credentials. Backup copies and previously delivered Telegram messages may remain until separately removed or expired under the relevant service or backup practices.

Your choices

The account owner can decline connections, revoke authorization through the relevant provider's account settings, and request access, correction, export, disconnection, or deletion by contacting the operator. Manual tracking remains available without a device connection. Provider-side data is managed directly with that provider.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the integration's data practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact Jacob Schroeder at jacobrschroeder@gmail.com.
